What goal 1 counts, and what it does not
-----------------------------------------
- The spec excludes the size-limit checkpoint pause from the four-request goal and asks for
  its value on its own. Measured: the longest small commit that a maintenance run held back
  waited 338 ms [337 ms] = 5.3 small requests. This is the longest wait of all small commits.
- In this workload the generation switch never came in an app commit. Each 10% change
  (about 102 MiB of WAL) passed the 64 MiB WAL size limit, and the maintenance run did the
  checkpoint and the switch. So the commit that waits for the seal-start record, the head
  move and the WAL header record is the maintenance-held commit above (the switch's extra
  wait was at most 137 ms [136 ms]). No small commit outside a maintenance run did a switch.
- The 10% change commit itself waits for its own log upload of about 102 MiB: 2.77 s
  [2.71 s]. That is one large request, not a small one; the spec's goal is about small ones.
- Small commits: 104 per run, median 64 ms [65 ms]. 5 generation switches, 2 maintenance
  runs, 0 skipped. The writer's packer thread was on (the default); it made no pack run
  during the 50 ms commits (its first check is after 60 s).

Restore detail (goal 2)
-----------------------
- buckwal::restore returned at 250 ms [282 ms]: 7 GETs, 3 log objects, 1 MiB block fetched.
- The writer open of the restored directory returned at 524 ms [584 ms], with blocks not yet
  local (Choice C). The background fetch made the file fully local (app.db-blocks removed) at
  3.35 s [3.30 s] from the restore start. All 262,144 rows were there.

Pack run detail (goal 3), step times of run 1
---------------------------------------------
- 3a, kept copy: read 554 ms, apply 1,451 ms, upload 8,776 ms, verify 59 ms, publish 83 ms;
  210 MB down, 1,076 MB up.
- 3b, new pack directory: read 87 ms, apply 4,255 ms, upload 8,795 ms, verify 64 ms,
  publish 86 ms; 1,286 MB down, 1,076 MB up.
- The upload of the 1 GB pack takes about 8.8 s at this store's speed (about 122 MB/s).

Two packers (goal 4)
--------------------
- Both runs started together from two new pack directories. One run moved the pointer
  (24.4 to 25.4 s); the other ended as superseded and deleted its own pack and codes.
- GC with a 5 s trim grace and pack keep time: 2 passes in 11 s. After it, pack/ held only
  the newest pack and its codes; the bucket held 7 objects: head, pack-latest, the pack, its
  codes and 3 log objects of the head's generation. No pages/ or manifest/ object, no orphan
  pack, no open multipart upload aborted.

Limits of this measurement
--------------------------
- Local RustFS, not a cloud store: the delay is per request, the bandwidth is local
  (about 122 MB/s up). On AWS S3 from this Mac the upload speed was 12.4 MB/s
  (docs/packer-recon.md), so a 1 GB pack upload there takes about 80 s, and goal 3 on such a
  link misses 30 s. Real AWS runs belong to the coordinator and were not made.
- The size-limit checkpoint pause was measured with no reader open. With readers that stay
  open, the pause can be longer (the README states the measured value).
